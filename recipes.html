Chocolate Chip Cookies

Makes about 24 • 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 225g soft butter
  • 150g white sugar
  • 165g brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla essence
  • 250g cake flour
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of sode
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 300g chocolate chips

Method

  1. Heat oven to 180°C
  2. Cream butter and both sugars together until fluffy
  3. Beat in eggs and vanilla
  4. Mix in flour, bicard, and salt, then fold in chocolate chips
  5. Scoop onto a lined baking tray and bake for 10 to 12 minutes
