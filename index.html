A little about me

Almost graduated, fully caffeinated.

I'm finishing my computer science degree and want to head into cybersecurity, where i get to think like a puzzle-solver and protect people at the same time. Outside of code, you'll find me either baking, playing the piano, dancing, reading, cuddling my dogs, or filming my day for my vlogs.

I'm a final-year Bachelor Computer and Information Sciences in Application Development student at Emeris (The IIE). I aspire to go into a career in Cybersecurity. My decision to choose Computer Science started in my matric year (2023). I went to an Open Day at Varsity College, how known as Emeris where i then attended College in 2024. In first year we had a module based on security, and thats where my interest and love for Cybersecurity started. Right now i plan to either do a postgrad at Nelson Mandela University completely based on Cybersecurity, or get into a graduate program where i can get expose to networking and cybersecurity.
